Marlow is appointed for full and partial renovations — kitchens, baths, additions, and whole-home overhauls — where structural work and design decisions need to move together, not in sequence. Every project begins with an honest read of what's actually there before anything gets torn out.

  • Structural renovations, additions, and whole-home overhauls
  • Kitchen and bath renovation, coordinated with licensed contractors
  • Permitting and code compliance managed on your behalf
  • Material and fixture specification integrated from the first plan
  • Construction oversight through final punch-list

Working within what's already there

Every renovation starts with what's actually behind the walls, not what the drawings assume. We open up enough to understand the real structure, the real systems, before committing to a plan — the surprises get found in week one, not month three.

Permitting handled, not just explained

Permits and inspections are managed directly with the municipality and your contractor, not left for you to chase. You get the outcome — approved plans, passed inspections — without sitting in the waiting room for it.

A studio you can speak to

The person who takes the first call is the same person who signs off on the last install. There's no handoff to a junior team once the contract is signed — that continuity protects both the design intent and your time.

How a renovation unfolds

Our work moves through clear stages: assessment, permitting, demolition, construction, and finishing. At each stage we present real samples and layouts so decisions are made with full understanding, never under pressure. You always know where the project stands and what it means for cost and timeline.

Built to last, not just to look finished

A renovation that only looks good on handover day isn't finished — it's deferred. We favor solid materials and correct structural work over anything that needs redoing in five years, even when it isn't the part anyone photographs.

Working with the wider team

Renovation is a collaborative act. We coordinate structural engineers, licensed contractors, and specialty tradespeople, and manage the relationship with permitting and inspections on your behalf. A well-run project is as much about clear communication between everyone involved as it is about the work itself.
